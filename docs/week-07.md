# Week 7

**Goal this week:** Find a reference design, get expert input on the pond, and start a first physical prototype.

## What we did

- **30/08/2026:** Looked for similar aquatic cleaning robots and found Clearbot, which has a very professional build. The Clearbot Neo is about three metres long, driven by a solar-battery-powered electric motor, and cleans designated sections of water systematically, much like a household robot cleaner.
- **31/08/2026:** Visited the Marine Biology department at CUSAT, Pallimukku. They put me in touch with a research scholar from the department. She was not available to meet in person, so I spoke with her by phone and sent the photos and details of the pond I had collected earlier.
- **31/08/2026:** Visited two electronics shops (Tomson and Matha Electronics) to source parts. I did not find a waterproof 12V motor or a propeller. I did find a 12V 85 RPM worm-gear motor in stock.
- **01/09/2026:** Built a first boat prototype from PVC boards, following the Clearbot design.

## Problems and blockers

- No waterproof 12V motor or propeller available in the local shops.
- The scholar could not meet in person, so the conversation had to happen by phone and text.

## Decisions

- Use Clearbot as the reference design for the build.

## Next week

- Get advice on what material to build the hull from.
- Share more details of the project with the scholar and get her feedback.
